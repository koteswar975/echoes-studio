Echoes Studio

Echoes Studio is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. The owner uses it to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not a public service and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules the owner's videos, and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists.
  • Reads the channels' own statistics so the owner can see how videos are doing.
  • Reads comments on the owner's videos and posts the owner's replies.

The channels it manages

  • InfraNarrative: cloud and DevOps explainers.
  • Ancient Echoes: calm history videos.

Why Google shows this page

When the owner connects a channel, Google asks them to allow Echoes Studio to use YouTube on their behalf, and links here so they can see what the app is. The app uses YouTube API Services only for the channels listed above.
