Privacy policy

Echoes Studio · last updated 17 November 2026

Echoes Studio is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. Only its owner signs in to it, to manage their own YouTube channels. It has no public sign-up and collects no data from visitors to this page.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to work with the owner's own channels:

  • the channels' videos, playlists, thumbnails and captions, to upload and manage them;
  • the channels' statistics and reports, to show the owner how videos are doing;
  • comments on the channels' videos, to show them to the owner and post the owner's replies.

It does not access any other Google account or any other person's private data.

Where the data is kept

  • The sign-in that Google issues is stored encrypted on the owner's computer. It is never published or shared.
  • Channel statistics and comments are stored on the owner's computer.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Other services the app uses

To draft replies and reports for the owner, the text of public comments on the owner's videos and the channels' statistics may be processed by Anthropic's Claude API. The owner's Google sign-in is never sent to any other service.

Removing access and data

  • The owner can disconnect a channel inside the app, which deletes the stored sign-in.
  • Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Data stored on the owner's computer is deleted by the owner.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the About page of either channel: InfraNarrative or Ancient Echoes.
